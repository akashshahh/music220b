Conversation

Conversation is a dialogue between a pianist and a laptop. Presented as a first-person recording, the piece captures a real-time loop of a pianist taking turns "talking" with the system to create an improvised musical statement. The program analyzes the pitch, volume, and density of what is played, then it answers with ambient textures that match what it just heard. Visuals on the laptop pulse and glow in response. The perspective puts the viewer on the piano bench, between the acoustic instrument and its digital counterpart.
