Music 220b - HW3: Breakcore Drum Machine

Akash Shah

Final

The core sequencer is the same : 16 steps at sixteenth-note intervals (~170 BPM), where each drum instrument has a pattern of velocity values that control dynamics (ghost notes at lower velocities, accents at higher ones). Each instrument has 4 preset patterns swappable live, with slight random pitch/velocity variation per hit. The mutation system flips 2-4 random steps to evolve grooves, and full randomize regenerates everything from scratch with per-instrument density targets. The pad synth is 7 voices of detuned saws mixed with noise through a sweeping LPF, playing big 13th chords through a shuffleable progression.

New since the milestone: individual drum mute toggles let you strip instruments in and out live. Sidechain compression ducks the pad synth against any selected drum pattern (the duck depth scales with hit intensity so harder hits pump more). The drop functionn syncs to the next downbeat, then runs a 4-bar noise riser (bandpass filter sweeping). After the riser peaks, everything goes silent for a bit , then the drop hits on the 1 with a crash and a hard reset of all patterns back to defaults.

In the recording I'm using all of the new features. You can hear me towards the end of the recording mutating every measure in succession lol
